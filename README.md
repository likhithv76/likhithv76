## About Me

I am a **Software Engineer and Technical Trainer** with a strong focus on **QA tooling, automation, and scripting**. I work closely with clients and students to build **reliable test systems**, **developer tooling**, and **practical training workflows** that scale effectively.

My approach emphasizes:
- **Clarity and maintainability** in code and documentation
- **Security-first** practices in automation and tooling
- **Real-world context** when teaching technical concepts
- **Practical solutions** that solve actual problems

I'm passionate about building tools that make testing and development more efficient, and about helping students understand not just *how* to code, but *why* certain approaches work better in production environments.
